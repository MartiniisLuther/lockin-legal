LockIn is a focus timer. It works fully offline. Nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on Cloud Sync or Buddies.

What we store

On your phone only (always): your focus sessions, streaks, credits, achievements, themes and settings.

In the cloud — only if you turn on Cloud Sync or Buddies:

DataWhy
A random account ID and your LockIn ID (e.g. LK-2609-7K3F)To identify your account
Focus statistics: totals, streaks, daily minutes / completed / failed, achievements, owned themesTo sync and back up your progress across your phones
Your last 100 sessions (start, end, length, result)Session history on all your phones
Your Google email address — only if you link GoogleSo you can sign in on another phone
Buddy connections and the nicknames you give buddies (visible only to you)Buddies
Your status (ready / focusing / offline, last seen)So buddies can see if you're available
Shared sessions, group codes, pingsTo run shared sessions — deleted automatically (see Retention)
A random ID per installationSo the same account can't run one shared session on two phones

We do not collect your name, contacts, location, advertising identifiers or analytics, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising. There are no ads or trackers in LockIn.

What buddies can see

Your LockIn ID, your status (ready / focusing / offline) and, on the leaderboard, your focus minutes and session counts for the current week or month. Only people you've accepted as buddies can see this.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), servers in [REGION — e.g. EU (Frankfurt)].
  • Google — only if you choose "Sign in with Google". Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process cloud data to provide the features you turn on (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract). Nothing is processed for marketing.

Retention

  • Shared sessions, group lobbies: deleted about 15 minutes after they end.
  • Pings: deleted after 1 day.
  • Session history in the cloud: your newest 100 sessions are kept.
  • Everything else: until you delete it.

Your rights and choices

  • Export: Profile → Export Focus Data (JSON or CSV).
  • Turn off sync: Profile → Cloud Sync → Turn off sync. Nothing is uploaded while it's off.
  • Delete: Profile → Danger Zone erases your data. [Account deletion: Profile → Delete Account.]
  • You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to processing. You may complain to a data-protection authority in your country.

Children

LockIn is not directed at children under 16 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
