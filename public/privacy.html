LockIn ("the app") is operated by Martin Lutaaya, Berlin, Germany ("we", "us").

LockIn can be used fully offline. Focus data and settings remain on your device unless you enable Cloud Sync or use features that require cloud processing, such as Buddies. If you link a Google account, Google authentication information is also processed as described below.

What We Store

On your phone only, while using LockIn offline: your focus sessions, streaks, credits, achievements, themes and in-app settings.

In the cloud, only when you enable Cloud Sync or use Buddies:

Data StoredReason For Storage
A random account ID and your LockIn ID (e.g. LK-0000-0A0A)To identify your account.
Focus statistics, including totals, streaks, daily minutes, completed and failed sessions, achievements and owned themesTo sync and back up your progress across your devices.
Your newest 100 sessions, including start time, end time, length and result To provide session history across your devices.
Your Google account identifier and email address - only if you link Google To connect your Google sign-in with your LockIn account and allow you to sign in again.
Buddy connections and the nicknames you assign to buddies. To provide the Buddies feature. The nicknames you assign are private to your account.
Your status, such as ready, focusing or offline, and last-seen information. So added buddies can see your availability for sessions.
Shared sessions, group codes, and pingsTo provide shared sessions and related social features. (see Retention)
A random ID for each installationTo help prevent the same account from running one shared session on multiple devices simultaneously.

We do not intentionally collect your contacts, precise location or advertising identifiers for LockIn. LockIn does not contain advertisements or third-party advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal data for advertising.

What Buddies can see

People you have accepted as buddies can see your LockIn ID and your current status, - ready, focusing or offline. Where a leaderboard is available, accepted buddies can also see the focus minutes and session counts shown for the relevant week or month. Your private buddy nicknames are not shown to the other person.

Service Providers

  • Supabase - database, authentication and cloud infrastructure. Our Supabase project is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt), Germany region. Supabase may use subprocessors and operational infrastructure in other locations where necessary to provide and secure its services.
  • Google - only if you choose “Sign in with Google”. Google provides the authentication service and processes information according to Google’s own privacy policy. LockIn receives the information necessary to associate your Google sign-in with your LockIn account.
  • Apple - LockIn is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple processes information associated with App Store use under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

International Transfers

Although our primary Supabase project is hosted in the EU, some processing by Supabase or its subprocessors may take place outside the selected hosting region. Where applicable, such transfers are subject to appropriate data-protection safeguards required by applicable law.

Legal Basis

We process cloud data that is necessary to provide Cloud Sync, account and Buddies functionality on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, because the processing is necessary to provide the features you request.

Where processing is necessary to protect the security and integrity of LockIn or prevent abuse, we may rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service.

We do not process your data for marketing or advertising purposes.

Retention

  • Shared sessions and group lobbies are deleted about 15 minutes after they end.
  • Pings are deleted after 1 day.
  • Cloud session history is limited to your newest 100 sessions.
  • Other cloud account and progress data is retained until you delete it or the account is otherwise terminated.
  • After account deletion, data is removed from our active systems. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or where retention is required by law, after which it is deleted or overwritten according to the applicable retention period.

Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you have the right to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority.

You can use the following LockIn functions where available:

  • Export: Profile → Export Focus Data (JSON or CSV).
  • Turn off Sync: Profile → Cloud Sync → Turn off sync. Session updates stay local, cloud data is not updated while sync is off.
  • Delete Focus Data: Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Focus Data (hold button for 5s). Erases all data on your account, streaks, credits, sessions, achievements and in-app settings. Both local and cloud data is deleted.
  • Delete Account: Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account (hold button for 5s). Erases account data including the account itself.

You can also contact us at lutaayamart10@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of your personal data or to exercise other applicable rights.

Children

LockIn is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children in connection with the service.

Changes
