LockIn can be used fully offline. Focus data and settings remain on your device unless you enable Cloud Sync or use features that require cloud processing, such as Buddies. If you sign in with Apple or Google, authentication information from that provider is also processed as described below.

What We Store

On your phone only, while using LockIn offline: your focus sessions, streaks, credits, achievements, themes, personal session tags and in-app settings.

In the cloud, only when you enable Cloud Sync or use Buddies:

Data StoredReason For Storage
A random account ID and your LockIn ID (e.g. LK-0000-0A0A)To identify your account.
Focus statistics, including totals, streaks, daily minutes, completed and failed sessions, sessions completed with buddies and in groups, achievements and owned themesTo sync and back up your progress across your devices.
Your newest 100 sessions, including start time, end time, length, result, session type (solo, buddy or group), the private nickname of the buddy you focused with, the group size, and any tag you add (e.g. "Studying biology") To provide session history across your devices. Tags and nicknames are private to your account.
Your Apple or Google account identifier and the email address the provider shares - only if you sign in with Apple or Google. With Apple's "Hide My Email", we only receive a private relay address. To connect your sign-in with your LockIn account and allow you to sign in again on other devices.
Buddy connections and the nicknames you assign to buddies. To provide the Buddies feature. The nicknames you assign are private to your account.
Your status, such as ready, focusing or offline, and last-seen information. So added buddies can see your availability for sessions.
Shared sessions, group codes, and pingsTo provide shared sessions and related social features. (see Retention)
A random ID for each installation (created by the app, not a hardware identifier) and that installation's contribution to your totalsSo progress from several devices adds up correctly, and to prevent the same account from running a shared session on multiple devices simultaneously.

If you contact us through "Report a problem" or "Send feedback", we receive your email address, your message and the details pre-filled in it (app version, iOS version and your LockIn ID). We use them only to answer you and fix problems.

We do not intentionally collect your contacts, precise location or advertising identifiers for LockIn. LockIn does not contain advertisements or third-party advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal data for advertising.

What Buddies can see

People you have accepted as buddies can see your LockIn ID and your current status, - ready, focusing or offline. Where a leaderboard is available, accepted buddies can also see the focus minutes and session counts shown for the relevant week or month. Your private buddy nicknames and session tags are never shown to anyone else.

During a shared session, the people in it can see that the session is running, whether you have stepped away from the app, and whether you left early. Group members see the group code, the number of people focusing and the host's LockIn ID (or the nickname they gave the host) — not the identities of other members.

Legal Basis

We process cloud data that is necessary to provide Cloud Sync, account and Buddies functionality on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, because the processing is necessary to provide the features you request.

Where processing is necessary to protect the security and integrity of LockIn or prevent abuse, we may rely on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service.

We do not process your data for marketing or advertising purposes.

Retention

  • Shared sessions and group lobbies are deleted about 15 minutes after they end.
  • Pings are deleted after 1 day.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Cloud session history is limited to your newest 100 sessions.
  • Other cloud account and progress data is retained until you delete it or the account is otherwise terminated.
  • After account deletion, data is removed from our active systems. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or where retention is required by law, after which it is deleted or overwritten according to the applicable retention period.

Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you have the right to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority.

You can use the following LockIn functions where available:

  • Export: Profile → Export Focus Data (JSON or CSV).
  • Turn off Sync: Profile → Cloud Sync → Turn off sync. Session updates stay local and cloud data is not updated while sync is off. Your account and Buddies keep working.
  • Disconnect this phone: Profile → Cloud Sync → Disconnect this phone (accounts linked to Apple or Google). This phone leaves your account and stops syncing; its data stays on the phone.
  • Erase Focus Data: Profile → Danger Zone (hold for 5 seconds). Erases your streaks, credits, sessions, achievements and in-app settings on this phone and in the cloud. Your account itself remains.
  • Delete Account: Profile → Danger Zone → Delete account (confirm by typing DELETE). Permanently deletes your account and all its cloud data — progress, session history, buddy connections, shared sessions and groups you host — on every device, and resets this phone.

You can also contact us at lutaayamart10@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of your personal data or to exercise other applicable rights.
