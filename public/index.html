Made for moments like these

Whatever you’re putting off, start here.

FocusLink helps you turn intentions into focused time. Work on your own, bring a buddy along, or get a group together. Every completed session builds momentum, strengthens your streak and brings you closer to your goals.

Study

“I need to study, but I keep checking my phone.”

Set a focus session, put distractions aside and give your studies the time they deserve. Start with 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or challenge yourself to go longer. Finish what you started and build momentum.

Deep Work

“I need to get this project done.”

Whether it's coding, writing, planning or tackling a deadline, block out time and commit to the task. No endless postponing. Just you, your work and a session to complete.

Accountability

“I work better when someone else is working too.”

Invite a buddy to a shared focus session. See when they're ready, start together and keep each other accountable. Sometimes knowing someone is right there makes all the difference.

Group Work

“Let’s all get some work done together.”

Bring your friends – or anyone with the code – into a focus room. Create a group lobby, share the code and let everyone join. No need to add everyone as a buddy first – just get together and get to work.

Daily Discipline

“I want to stop saying I’ll do it tomorrow.”

Make showing up a habit. Complete sessions, build your streak and earn Focus Points along the way. Small commitments add up to meaningful progress.

Solo or Together

One goal. Three ways to focus.

Whether you prefer working independently or alongside others, FocusLink gives you the flexibility to focus your way. Start a solo session, team up with a buddy, or bring everyone into a group session.

Solo

Your time. Your focus.

Choose 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or set your own duration up to five hours. Start, stay focused and finish.

1:1 with a buddy

Show up for each other.

Invite a ready buddy and start a shared session together. Stay accountable, keep each other on track and finish what you committed to. If either person leaves early, the shared session fails for both.

Group

Bring everyone together.

Create a group lobby and share the code so anyone can join. No buddy connection required. Everyone focuses together, while each participant is responsible for completing their own session.

How FocusLink works

Start. Stay. Finish.

FocusLink rewards only completed focus. Start a session, stay in it, and finish what you committed to.

✓

Lock your screen

Put your phone away if you want. Your session keeps running on the Lock Screen.

✕

Leave the session

Leave FocusLink during an active session and you have 10 seconds to come back. Don’t return in time? Your session fails. Leave too often and you earn fewer points.

★

Finish it

Complete 15+ minutes and you build your streak and earn Focus Points for the Vault.

Buddies

Your circle stays personal.

Buddies are the people you can directly connect with for 1:1 focus sessions and compare your progress with. Group sessions are different: they can be open to anyone with the lobby code.

  • See who's ready, focusing or offline
  • Send a ping when you're ready to focus together
  • Invite a buddy who’s ready (and not already in a session) to a 1:1 session
  • Compare your progress on weekly and monthly leaderboards
Rewards

Your rewards come from doing the work.

Focus Points are earned by completing sessions. Spend them in the Vault to customize your FocusLink app.

  • Unlock themes and timer rings with your Focus Points
  • Use them on a Streak Freeze or Streak Restore for days when a session doesn’t go to plan

iPhone & iPad

One focus space. Across your devices.

FocusLink is designed for iPhone and iPad. Keep your sessions, history, activity heatmap and unlocked rewards in sync across devices.

Private by design

Focus should stay focused.

FocusLink doesn’t need to know everything about you to help you work. Your data is stored offline by default. Turn on Cloud Sync to back up your progress and use it across devices. Buddy connections require Cloud Sync.

Works offline

No account required to start. Your session history can stay entirely on your device.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising IDs, no analytics SDKs, and no selling your data.

Sync when you want

Sign in with Apple or Google when you want your progress across devices.

You’re in control of your data

Export your data, erase it, or delete your account directly from the app.
