How it works

One simple rule makes it work.

Every session is a small commitment. FocusLink adds just enough friction to help you keep it.

✓

Lock your screen

Totally fine. The timer keeps going on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

✕

Leave the app

A short countdown starts. Not back in time? The session fails and your streak resets.

★

Finish it

Sessions of 15+ minutes grow your streak and earn Focus Points for the Vault.

Solo or together

Three ways to focus.

Go it alone, link up with one buddy, or open a room for a group. Every theme you unlock comes with you.

Solo

On your own

Pick 15, 30, 60 minutes or any length up to five hours. Just you and the timer.

1:1

With a buddy

Invite a friend. You both get a short countdown and start together — if either of you leaves, it ends for both.

Group

In a group

Share a code. It starts when two or more are in. Leaving only ends your own session.

Buddies

A small circle, not a social network.

Add people with their FocusLink ID — no contacts, no feed, no followers.

  • See who's ready, focusing or offline
  • Send a ping when you're ready to focus
  • Weekly and monthly leaderboard with your buddies
  • Private nicknames, and hide anyone at any time
Rewards

  • Achievements from Stone to Platinum — never taken away once earned
